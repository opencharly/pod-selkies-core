# 2026.242.2119 — chore(selkies-core): advance 11 stale pins so published tags carry current refs

## Summary

**11 of this candy's 17 pinned refs** were behind their repos' newest tags. That matters
beyond tidiness: a published tag embeds the pins it carried at tag time, so every consumer
pinning a tag of this candy drags those old refs into its own resolution, and charly reports
them as `resolved to multiple versions` on every build.

Plus this candy's own CalVer (`2026.242.1310` → `2026.242.2050`), because changed content

### Why the fix belongs here rather than in the consumer

Measured in `distro-cachyos`, the surviving conflict

```
ignoring 2026.187.1200 (from github.com/opencharly/plugin-wl@v2026.237.1430)
```

resolves through a **published tag** of this candy — even though this candy's `main` already
pinned `plugin-wl` at the newest version. The stale ref lives in the tag, not in main.

Advancing the consumer's pin instead does not help and measurably hurts. Every option tried
there, on one tree:

| Change in distro-cachyos | Warnings |
|---|---|
| baseline | **2** |
| advance `pod-selkies-core` alone | 6 |
| advance whole selkies family | 8 |
| advance ALL 23 stale pins (of 177) | 8 |

Advancing a consumer to a newer tag simply exposes that tag's *other* stale refs. The only
change that removes an edge is the one made at its source, so the next tag cut here carries
current pins throughout. Five such source-level fixes landed today —
`layer-chrome-sway#2`, `layer-sway-desktop#3`, `layer-xfce4-terminal#3`,
`layer-sway-desktop-vnc#4`, `pod-charly-automation#4` — each taking its own gate from exit 1
to exit 0.

Context and the full measurement set: opencharly/distro-cachyos#48.
